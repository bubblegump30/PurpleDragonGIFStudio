# Community Code of Conduct

Purple Dragon Foundation Ltd welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Our standards

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Discuss ideas and code without attacking people. Welcome newcomers, respect different experiences, and keep feedback useful and relevant.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, spam, and publishing someone else's private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not post API keys, credentials, private media, or personal information.

These standards apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project community spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

For public spam or abusive content, use GitHub's reporting tools and alert the maintainer, @bubblegump30, without repeating sensitive content. For a private report, use a private contact method listed on [our website](https://www.purpledragonfoundationltd.xyz/). If none is available, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without naming affected people or sharing details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, issue warnings, or restrict participation depending on the severity and recurrence of the behavior. Maintainers will consider context and handle reports with discretion; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Community members and maintainers are expected to follow these standards.
